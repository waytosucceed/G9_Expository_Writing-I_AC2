Key Takeaways


  1. What is expository writing?
    Expository writing is a type of writing that explains, informs, or clarifies a topic without adding personal opinions. It presents facts and information in a clear, organised way.
  2. What is an advice sheet, and what is its purpose?
    An advice sheet is a type of expository writing that gives practical, helpful guidance to a specific audience. Its purpose is to inform and guide readers through a situation.
  3. What is the structure of an advice sheet?
    An advice sheet consists of a clear title, an introduction that states the topic and why the advice matters, 3-5 advice points in the body (each focused on one specific piece of advice), and a conclusion.
  4. Why does the title matter?
    The title matters because it's the first thing readers see that tells them what to expect. It makes them want to continue reading.
  5. How do we start the introduction?
    We begin the introduction by acknowledging their feelings/concerns/problems (the topic of the advice sheet).

Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Prosaic
Meaning: Ordinary, unimaginative, lacking poetic beauty; also practical or straightforward.
Example: The report was thorough but written in a rather prosaic style.

Sagacious
Meaning: Wise, shrewd, showing keen mental discernment and good judgment.
Example: Her sagacious counsel helped the team avoid a costly mistake.

Compendium
Meaning: A concise collection or summary of knowledge, often in book or manual form.
Example: The handbook served as a compendium of essential medical practices.
